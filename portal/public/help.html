Need a hand?

Quick answers for connecting and paying.

How do I get online?

Choose a plan, enter your mobile-money number and approve the request on your phone. Access starts after payment is confirmed.

Can I reconnect later?

Yes. Reconnect with the same device while your plan is active. Your subscription time continues even when Wi-Fi is off.

My payment is pending

Check your phone for an approval prompt. Don’t pay again while the first request is pending. If it remains pending, contact the compound owner.

I changed my phone or Wi-Fi address

Plans are linked to one device. Ask the owner to move your active plan to your new device.

Terms & fair use

Access is for the subscribed device. Refund or payment disputes should be raised with the compound owner.
